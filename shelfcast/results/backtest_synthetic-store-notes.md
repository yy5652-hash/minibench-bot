# ShelfCast backtest: synthetic-store-notes

The synthetic store with one addition: in about 8% of weeks something happened that the planner knew about and wrote down in a free-text note (a short delivery, a viral post, a rival closed, roadworks). The note names no number. Generated by shelfcast.data.generate_synthetic; not real sales.

- Forecasts: **520** (20 series × last 26 weeks, 2026-04-13 to 2026-10-05), one week ahead
- Model: `Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507`, 5 samples per forecast; LLM answered 520/520
- Economics: margin 35% of price, leftover cost 25% of unit cost (newsvendor order at the critical ratio)
- Generated 2026-10-16T05:45:34+00:00

- Prompt tokens 877,526, completion tokens 630,859
- Wall time 116.7s, mean request latency 14.436s, 40 responses served from cache

Scaled pinball is the mean quantile loss over 11 levels divided by the SKU's mean weekly demand (lower is better). FVA is the relative improvement of the calibrated agent over the calibrated statistical baseline, so calibration is never credited to the model. Calibrated variants are fit only on earlier weeks.
